# Version and selection notes

- **Sprint submission:** 26 programmed conditions, repeated in a fresh environment on the same host. The original manuscript and `stop-scope-supplement-v1.0.0` tag remain historical artifacts.
- **1 October revision, manuscript v1.1:** reorganized exposition and related work; not a new experimental sample.
- **9 October evidence snapshot:** selected live development episodes and local controls, preserved at its own path.
- **16 October publication, manuscript v1.2:** integrates the original comparison with a complete five-arm live persistence comparison and a provider-free, three-timing-by-four-control receiver matrix plus four faults. These are saved research results, not new live calls made for publication.
- **Component package versions:** live persistence evidence v0.1.0 and receiver reproducer v0.1.0. Their independent versions do not reset the manuscript version.

The main table includes all five conditions of the selected persistence comparison, including controls that do not prevent effects. It does not select only successful controls. The receiver package includes all 16 registered cells, including its intentionally incomplete receipt chain. Other development packages are represented only in the explicitly labelled coverage assessment; they are not pooled as independent observations.

The 286-case map is a projection of the 15 October assessment. Original case IDs and identity hashes, observed scope, remaining work and retained evidence identifiers are preserved. Omitted fields are the longer repeated arm/obligation templates and raw witness lists. No original registry promotion, new author approval, new live episode or new formal case acceptance is performed by this export.

Future revisions should keep model-behaviour tests, control-mechanism tests, independent replication and catalogue qualification as separate evidence claims.
