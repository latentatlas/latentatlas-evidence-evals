# Evidence Authority After Retrieval — open framework source

Original paper: DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21243387; v0.2 (7 July 2026).
The published PDF and available author manuscript source are supplied.
This paper proposes a design framework; it reports no new empirical dataset.
No dataset or experiment is invented for the open-data release.

Related open reference modules can be inspected in the companion
`../authority-to-action/source/latentatlas/` directory: evidence qualification,
indexing and action-time revalidation. Their snapshot date and scope are
separate from the framework manuscript. They are not an empirical validation
of this paper. Author-owned text: CC BY 4.0; companion original code: MIT.
