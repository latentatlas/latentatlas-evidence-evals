# Authority Leases — open masked study and protocol

Original paper: DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21432540 (18 July 2026).
The manuscript source is byte-identical to the published version.

- `outputs/latentatlas/action_time_p0_review_freeze_v1/`: all 146 reviewed, masked action-event rows, outcomes and denominator records.
- `outputs/latentatlas/action_time_masked_evidence_adapter_p0_full/`: the full masked input cohort, all 151 high-priority packets (including the five excluded before review), response sheet and correction record.
- `outputs/latentatlas/action_time_pattern_cluster_review_p0_full/`: pattern-level review and propagation records; these are not additional independent reviewers.
- `outputs/latentatlas/action_time_p0_reviewer_pack_v1/`: masked packets, label guide and review instrument.
- `outputs/latentatlas/concurrency_closure_pack_20260718/`: 27-field measurement contract, 12 synthetic fixtures, baseline replay, disclosure, overlap and summaries.
- `outputs/latentatlas/action_time_p0_figure_table_pack_v3/`: all five figures and publication tables.
- `research/latentatlas_evidence_benchmark/`: original study/protocol builders.

The initial high-priority denominator is 151; five pre-review insufficient
packets remain excluded from the 146 reviewed rows. The supplied masked
research representation does not contain raw product/customer source systems.
It preserves the study's recorded evidence boundary. Replay reads review
labels and is diagnostic, not an independent prospective policy evaluation.

Local user-home prefixes in bookkeeping summaries are replaced by `/LOCAL_HOME`.
`SOURCE_PROVENANCE.json` records original and public-copy hashes. The numerical
findings and original PDF are unchanged; historical internal hashes identify
the original source bytes rather than the transformed bookkeeping copy.

The existing open implementation is in the companion Authority-to-Action
source (`latentatlas/action_time_revalidation.py`). It is a reference
implementation, not evidence of production effectiveness of this study.
