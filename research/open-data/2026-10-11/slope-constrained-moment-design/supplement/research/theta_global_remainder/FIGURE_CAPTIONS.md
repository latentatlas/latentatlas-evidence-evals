# R26 — Scientific figure caption

**Uniform fourth-order error and adaptive tail coverage at a fixed theta
cusp.** Left: the bound 100·1.488×10⁻⁵³/(C₄,lower M²), with
C₄,lower=2.49203004×10⁻³⁹, is an upper bound on the percentage error
of P₄(M)=δ₀+C₂/M²+C₄/M⁴ relative to its fourth-order term. It is valid
for every M≥2×10⁻⁵; the graphic displays a finite window up to M=20.
Gray marks the earlier R25 validity window, not a change of formula.
Right: max{1,¼ log(εM/δ₀,lower)}, ε=2⁻¹², bounds the coordinate of
every root included in the adaptive feasible construction. This follows
from a=A₊/M≥δ₀/M and the criterion ae^{4z}≤ε. It is neither an exact
root count nor a bound on all zeros or on the support of the kernel.
The first 28 roots below 1 are always retained. Both curves are bounds
derived from the proof; no sampled optimal values δ(M) are plotted.
Exact coefficients are used in the theorem. Decimal coefficient errors
are not included. No sixth-order coefficient is asserted.
