# R22 — Scientific figure caption

**Fourth-order amplitude cost and its regularity boundary.** The plotted
quantity is M⁴[δ(M)−δ₀−C₂/M²] for three compact moment-design examples
with δ₀=1/4. (a) For q₁=(x²−1/4)(1+x/4), the curve converges to
C₄=253/49152. (b) For q₁=x−3x³+9x⁵, it converges to C₄=−1/15360.
Thus the fourth-order coefficient has no universal sign. (c) For the
C² density q₁=x+sgn(x)|x|⁵ᐟ², the curve grows as √M/6336+O(1);
there is no finite fourth-order limit. Dashed lines indicate the two
limits and the leading divergent term. Dark points have outward
rational enclosures. Curves were sampled at 80 decimal digits and
rendered as illustrations, not used as proof. Horizontal axes are
logarithmic. These examples are not new theta-family certificates.
