# R21 — Scientific figure caption

**Exact finite-slope moment design in a two-switch polynomial example.**
On [-1,1], q₀=1, q₁=(x²−1/4)(1+x/4), and the target moment is 1/8.
(a) The unit profile of the unique optimum at half-width a=0.2. Orange
points mark the two ramp centers; shaded intervals are the ramps. The
dotted profile is the unrestricted sign solution. (b) The common center
shift d(a) and dual coefficient b(a); dotted curves show −a²/8 and
61a²/192. At this scale the leading curves nearly coincide with the exact
curves. (c) The scaled amplitude excess M²[δ(M)−1/4] tends to 1/48.
Six dark markers have exact rational enclosures; the drawn curve is a
floating-point illustration of the closed parametrization. Horizontal
axis in (c) is logarithmic. The example validates the compact,
finite-switch theorem; it is not a new certificate for the theta family.
