# R16 figure captions

## Figure 1 — Explicit relative remainder envelope

[PNG](figures/remainder_envelope.png), [SVG](figures/remainder_envelope.svg).

For M≥M₀=0.00002, the normalized excess deviation
[δ(M)−δ*]/[C*/M²]−1 is enclosed between −K₋/(C_lower M) and
K₊/(C_lower M). The vertical coordinate is percent. The shaded
region is an analytic inequality, not a sampled optimal curve or a
statistical confidence interval. The hollow point is a separate
numerical ramp witness at M₀; its amplitude is not asserted to be
the exact finite-M minimum. The certified family is defined by the
quantitative contraction proof, not by that numerical residual.

Sources: `results/remainder_check.json`, `results/design_crosscheck.json`.

## Figure 2 — Improvement of the enclosing interval at M₀

[PNG](figures/threshold_comparison.png), [SVG](figures/threshold_comparison.svg).

The R14 and R16 certified bounds are expressed as parts per million
of excess over δ*, using the same inherited δ* bounds. Panel B is an
explicitly labeled zoom. The R16 displayed total-threshold interval
is over 870 times narrower than R14. This is improved enclosure
precision, not a change of model, objective, physical efficiency or
the true mathematical minimum.

Source: `results/remainder_check.json` and the frozen R14/R12 displayed
brackets. Both figures concern the same exact Q and original u slope.
