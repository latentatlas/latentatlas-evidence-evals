\section{Pinned-cusp shape design in a positive kernel family}

Let $F(t;\lambda,\mu,\nu)$ be the theta-kernel integral of the paper,
and let $Q=(t_Q,\lambda_Q,\mu_Q)$, $\nu=0$, be the exact certified
zero of $F_0=F_1=F_2=0$, with $F_3>0>F_4$. Subscripts denote
$t$ derivatives. For $1\leq j\leq16$, let $M_{nj}$ be the exact
$n$th derivative moment at $Q$ with the multiplier $\cos(2ju)$.
For the four columns $J=(10,13,15,16)$ of the three-row moment
matrix, define $c_i=(-1)^{i-1}\det M_{\widehat i}$,
$Z=\sum_i|c_i|$, and $w_i=\sigma c_i/Z$, where $\sigma=\pm1$
chooses the positive objective below. Put
\[
 h_*(u)=\sum_{i=1}^4w_i\cos(2J_i u),\qquad
 G_\epsilon=F+\epsilon H,
\]
where $H$ is the same integral with the multiplier $h_*$. All
cofactors are nonzero. The exact cofactor identity gives
$H_0(Q)=H_1(Q)=H_2(Q)=0$, while $\sum|w_i|=1$ gives
$\|h_*\|_\infty\leq1$. Thus $Q$ remains a zero of order at least
three, and $\Phi_\epsilon=\Phi(1+\epsilon h_*)>0$ for $|\epsilon|<1$.

\paragraph{Theorem 1 (finite-class optimum).}
Among $h=\sum_{j=1}^{16}v_j\cos(2ju)$ with three vanishing $Q$
moments and $\sum|v_j|\leq1$, the oriented vector $w$ is the unique
maximizer of $-\partial_\epsilon\log C(0)$, where $C$ is the
leading local fold-width coefficient in the original controls.
Writing $a=H_3(Q)/F_3(Q)$ and $b=H_4(Q)/F_4(Q)$, its value is
\[
 S=b-a\in[83.766976561083,83.766976561084],
\]
\[
 a\in[-53.279001512222,-53.279001512221],\qquad
 b\in[30.487975048861,30.487975048862].
\]
To certify the optimum, row-scale the constraint matrix to $A$,
put $d_j=M_{4j}/F_4-M_{3j}/F_3$, and solve exactly
$d_j-A_j^Ty=z\operatorname{sign}(w_j)$ on $J$. The computed
enclosures prove $z>0$ and $|d_j-A_j^Ty|<z$ off $J$, with
strict slack greater than $10.51$. Every feasible vector obeys
$d^Tv\leq z\|v\|_1\leq z$ and $w$ attains equality. Strict
off-support slack and rank three give uniqueness. This is an
optimum of the initial rate in the declared finite coefficient
class, not an optimum over all bounded perturbations or of a
finite fold width.

\paragraph{Theorem 2 (prescribed leading opening).}
For every $r>0$, the amplitude
\[
 \epsilon(r)=\frac{1-r}{rb-a}
\]
keeps $Q$ a nondegenerate cusp in the $(\lambda,\mu)$ controls,
satisfies $\Phi_\epsilon>0.9672\Phi$, and gives exactly
$C(\epsilon)=rC(0)$. Indeed
\[
 C(\epsilon)=\frac{8\sqrt2}{3}\frac{-G_3(Q)}{G_4(Q)},\qquad
 \frac{C(\epsilon)}{C(0)}=\frac{1+a\epsilon}{1+b\epsilon},
\]
and $1+a\epsilon=r(b-a)/(rb-a)>0$,
$1+b\epsilon=(b-a)/(rb-a)>0$. These amplitudes fill
$(-1/b,-1/a)$, whose endpoints satisfy
\[
 -1/b\in[-0.032799816925,-0.032799816924],\qquad
 -1/a\in[0.018769120509,0.018769120510].
\]
This concerns the leading coefficient in
$W=C\ell^{3/2}+O(\ell^2)$. No common finite window over that
whole open interval, or unbounded finite width at fixed $\ell$,
is asserted. $C$ depends on the original control units.

\paragraph{Theorem 3 (finite geometry and strict nesting).}
For every $|\epsilon|\leq1/128$, at $\nu=0$, use the exact
translations $s=t-t_Q$, $\ell=\lambda-\lambda_Q$, $m=\mu-\mu_Q$.
In the common window
\[
 |s|\leq0.003,\qquad |\ell|\leq10^{-6},\qquad |m|\leq2\,10^{-9},
\]
the discriminant consists of $Q$ and two folds
$m_-(\ell,\epsilon)<0<m_+(\ell,\epsilon)$ for
$0<\ell\leq10^{-6}$. Between them there are exactly three
simple real zeros in the stated $s$ window; off the discriminant
outside that strip there is exactly one. A noncusp fold has one
double and one simple zero; $Q$ has one triple zero. No zero
crosses the $s$ endpoints. Moreover
\[
 \partial_\epsilon m_+<0<\partial_\epsilon m_-,\qquad
 10\ell^{3/2}<-\partial_\epsilon(m_+-m_-)<500\ell^{3/2}.
\]
At $\ell=10^{-6}$, direct finite fold contractions give
\[
 100\left[\frac{W(+1/128)}{W(-1/128)}-1\right]
 \in[-74.63952481,-74.63952480]\quad\text{percent}.
\]
The old four-mode direction at the same amplitudes instead gives
$[-0.00078034,-0.00078033]$ percent. The new leading-$C$
percentage change is $[-74.6395459500,-74.6395459499]$;
it is not substituted for the finite-width result.

Sixteen amplitude intervals and fifteen common-uniqueness-box
joins establish the continuum statement. The auxiliary fold
box has radii $2^{-13},2^{-19}$ in $(\ell,m)$.
Weighted contraction bounds satisfy $q+\eta<1$. The signs
$G_3>0>G_4$ and $\Delta=G_3G_4-G_2G_5<0$ yield the fold ODE
\[
 \ell_s=4G_2G_4/\Delta,\qquad m_s=16G_2^2/\Delta.
\]
At fixed $\ell$, $\partial_\epsilon m=B(s)=-16H_0/G_4$.
At $Q$, $B=B'=B''=0$,
$B'''=-32k_\epsilon>0$, and
$B''''=96k(A_3)_\epsilon$, where
$k=-G_3/G_4$ and $A_3=\frac43(G_5/G_4-G_4/G_3)$.
Verified fifth-derivative remainders keep $B'''(s)>0$ on
$|s|\leq0.001$, which covers all needed positive $\ell$.
The negative-$s$ fold is the upper branch; hence the signs above.

\paragraph{Theorem 4 (constructed order-four zero).}
At $\epsilon_4=-1/a$, the modified integral has an exact zero
of order four at $Q$, and its $(\lambda,\mu,\nu)$ unfolding has
local zero equation analytically equivalent to
\[
 x^4+ux^2+vx+w=0.
\]
The exact pinning identities and definition of $\epsilon_4$ give
$G_0=G_1=G_2=G_3=0$, and the certificate gives
\[
 10^{12}G_4\in[-1.527706119921,-1.527706119920].
\]
For the parameter Jacobian of $(G_0,G_1,G_2)$,
\[
 \det J_p=\frac{G_4(G_4G_7-G_5G_6)}{4096},\qquad
 10^{38}\det J_p\in[-1.247577850654,-1.247577850653].
\]
Weierstrass preparation factors $G$ into a nonvanishing unit
and a monic quartic. The rank-three jet matrix gives rank three
for its constant, linear and quadratic coefficient map. Removing
the cubic term preserves that rank, and the inverse function
theorem gives the displayed control coordinates. Its antiderivative
is the standard quintic swallowtail potential. At the other
endpoint $\epsilon_\mu=-1/b$, only $G_4$ vanishes: $G_3>0$
and the $(\lambda,\mu)$ cusp unfolding loses rank; the root
does not become fourth order.

\paragraph{Evidence and scope.}
The package records 288 rigorous frequency-shift integrals,
110 direct new $F/H$ integrals, 34 old-direction comparison
integrals, separate rational dual and rank checks, independent
rational reconstruction of all new local Taylor bounds, and
176 checked finite fold contractions. Nine direct moments also
agree under separate mpmath integration at two precisions.
The rational checkers assume the saved rigorous integral
enclosures and positive majorants; the sample checker also
assumes saved point Taylor enclosures. These are internal
independent implementations, not external peer review.
The new finite-window result is at $\nu=0$ and uses a changed
kernel. It does not prove an order-four zero for the original
undeformed kernel, a global discriminant, or physical stability.
