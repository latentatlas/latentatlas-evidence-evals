# R09 — Standard tools and reading scope

Accessed 20 September 2026. These sources support the standard arguments
and terminology, not the project-specific numerical claims or a claim
of literature priority. No full related-work search for R09 is asserted.

- Stephen Boyd and Lieven Vandenberghe, *Convex Optimization*, official
  Stanford EE364a lecture slides on duality. Read the linear-programming
  and weak-duality statements; use in R09: the elementary upper bound
  dᵀv≤z||v||₁ under Av=0 and ||d−Aᵀy||∞≤z.
  [Official slides](https://web.stanford.edu/class/ee364a/lectures/duality.pdf).
- Ngô Bảo Châu, *Weierstrass preparation theorem and singularities in the
  space of non-degenerate arcs*, section 3, printed page 5. Read the
  recalled classical holomorphic preparation statement, not the whole
  article. Use: nonvanishing analytic unit times a monic polynomial.
  [Author-hosted text](https://math.uchicago.edu/~ngo/Weierstrass.pdf).
- NIST Digital Library of Mathematical Functions, §36.2(i), equation
  36.2.1. Read the cuspoid potential normal forms and K=1,2,3 terminology.
  Use: the quintic potential with three controls is the swallowtail;
  its stationary equation is quartic. This does not identify our
  original integral with a canonical catastrophe integral.
  [NIST definitions](https://dlmf.nist.gov/36.2#i).

Earlier integral-family and validated-cusp literature remains recorded
in [R06](../cusp_literature/ARASTIRMA_NOTU.md). Its earlier scope should
not be mistaken for a priority search on the new R09 construction.
