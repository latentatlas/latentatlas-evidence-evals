\section{A certified near-minimum positive kernel modification}

Let $Q=(\tau,\lambda_Q,\mu_Q,0)$ be the exact R01 cusp of the original
theta kernel, and put
\[
 d\rho=\Phi(u)e^{\lambda_Qu^2+\mu_Qu^4}\,du,\quad
 p_j=(2u)^j\cos(2\tau u+j\pi/2),\quad
 f_j=\int p_j\,d\rho,\quad L_j(h)=\int p_jh\,d\rho.
\]
Thus $f_0=f_1=f_2=0$ and $f_3>0$. For a single real bounded measurable
multiplier $h$, independent of all moving controls, define
\[
 \delta_* =\inf\{\|h\|_\infty:
 L_0(h)=L_1(h)=L_2(h)=0,\ L_3(h)=-f_3\}.
\]
No frequency or derivative constraint is imposed.

\paragraph{Certified threshold and feasible geometry.}
The computer-assisted bounds are
\[
 \boxed{0.00000000091787079603827<\delta_*
             <0.00000000091787079608363.}
\]
For the displayed endpoints $L,U$, $(U-L)/L<5\times10^{-11}$.
An explicitly defined even real-analytic multiplier $h_c$ has
$\|h_c\|_\infty<U$, so $\Phi(1+h_c)>0$. It preserves the exact
point $Q$ and gives a zero of order exactly four with a rank-three
$(\lambda,\mu,\nu)$ unfolding. In particular
\[
10^{13}g_4\in[-9.721188538,-9.721188537],\qquad
10^{40}\det J\in[-4.249937772,-4.249937771].
\]

\paragraph{Lower certificate.}
For fixed rational $a_i$, put $r=p_3-\sum_{i=0}^2a_ip_i$.
Every admissible $h$ satisfies
$f_3=|\int rh\,d\rho|\le\|h\|_\infty\int|r|d\rho$.
The objective upper bound is certified by 28 residual-root boxes,
113 sign leaves covering their complement in $[0,1]$, signed
moment integrals on 29 intervals and an analytic infinite-domain
tail. Optimality of the candidate $a_i$ is not assumed.

\paragraph{Smooth feasible certificate.}
Let $s_0$ be the even alternating sign template with 28 positive
rational knots $b_k$, and define
\[
 H_\eta(v)=\tfrac12(1+\tanh(v/\eta)),\quad
 s_\eta(u)=1+\sum_{k=1}^{28}-2(-1)^{k-1}
        \{H_\eta(u-b_k)+H_\eta(-u-b_k)\},\quad \eta=2^{-32}.
\]
This is convolution by a nonnegative kernel of mass one, hence
$|s_\eta|\le1$. If the even extension of $q_j=\rho p_j$ has
Lipschitz constant $M_j$, odd-error cancellation gives
\[
 \left|\int_0^\infty q_j(s_\eta-s_0)\,du\right|
       \le28M_j\eta^2.
\]
The constants are bounded by positive theta-series derivative
envelopes on 256 dyadic cells and a monotone tail.
For the certified nonsingular R11 matrix
$A_{nj}=\int p_n\cos(2ju)d\rho$, $0\le n\le3$, $j=40,41,42,43$,
choose the exact rational
$\alpha=8877101635207251/9671406556917033397649408$ and define
\[
 Aw=(0,0,0,-f_3)^T+\alpha(L_0(s_\eta),\ldots,L_3(s_\eta))^T,
 \qquad h_c=-\alpha s_\eta+\sum_{j=40}^{43}w_j\cos(2ju).
\]
The four equalities are exact by this definition, and the norm is
bounded above by $\alpha+\sum|w_j|$. A rational adjugate calculation
and full $3\times3$ control determinant independently check the
algebra from the supplied integral enclosures.

\paragraph{Nonattainment in the continuous class.}
For the full-support positive density here, the R11 dual equality
condition forces every measurable minimizer to equal
$-(f_3/D)\operatorname{sgn}(r_{a_*})$ almost everywhere, where
$a_*$ is an L1 minimizer and $D=\int|r_{a_*}|d\rho$.
The analytic residual changes sign, so no continuous function can
equal these two opposite constants on the adjacent positive-density
intervals. Thus no continuous multiplier attains $\delta_*$.
The smooth positive nondegenerate class nevertheless has the same
infimum by R11, and the present design is within a relative factor
$1+5\times10^{-11}$ of it.

\paragraph{Scope.}
The norm controls amplitude only. Narrow smooth transitions need
not be small in a derivative norm. This is a new modified kernel;
R10's finite root windows are not transferred. The analytic proof
awaits external review, and no novelty is claimed for the underlying
duality or smoothing principles.
