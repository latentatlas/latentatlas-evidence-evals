# R10 — Standard geometric references and scope of reading

Accessed 20 September 2026.

- [NIST DLMF §36.2(i)](https://dlmf.nist.gov/36.2#i), equation 36.2.1:
  the cuspoid potentials and the K=3 swallowtail terminology. Used
  to distinguish a quintic potential from its quartic stationary
  equation. No identification of our integral with a canonical
  catastrophe integral is asserted.
- [NIST DLMF §36.4](https://dlmf.nist.gov/36.4), especially equations
  36.4.7–36.4.9: the standard swallowtail bifurcation surface, its
  self-intersection and its cusp arms. Read the formulas and their
  immediate definitions. The R10 finite numerical enclosures are
  project results, not statements imported from this source.
- [R09 standard-tool references](../cusp_shape_design/REFERENCES.md):
  the preparation theorem used for the previous germ result. R10's
  finite root count instead uses explicit derivative bounds, uniform
  contractions and Rolle/monotonicity arguments in original controls.

The current reading checks terminology and interpretation. It is not
a new comprehensive priority search for the kernel-design/finite-window
combination. The earlier related-work record is
[R06](../cusp_literature/ARASTIRMA_NOTU.md); R10 still needs its own
focused comparison before a manuscript novelty claim is finalized.
