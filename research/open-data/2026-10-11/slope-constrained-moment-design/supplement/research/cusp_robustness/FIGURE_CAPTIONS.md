# R07 figure captions

**Uniform kernel robustness.** Cellwise bounds for every fixed real
measurable relative kernel perturbation with essential supremum at most
10^-18. (A) Componentwise maximum-norm cusp displacement from the
unperturbed graph; the dashed line is the readable common upper bound.
(B) Interval bounds for −∂νW_h/ℓ^(3/2), uniform over 0<ℓ≤10^-6.
The shaded sets are computed enclosures on each driver interval, not
sampled actual widths. Every section uses its own perturbed exact cusp
as origin in the original unrescaled λ,μ coordinates. Data are from the
independent rational certificate check.
[PNG](figures/uniform_robustness.png), [SVG](figures/uniform_robustness.svg).

**A finite-amplitude direction fixing the exact quartic cusp.**
(A) The relative kernel multiplier 1+εh₀(u) for ε=±1/2, with the full
range of multipliers for |ε|≤1/2 shaded. The four cosine coefficients
are defined by exact integral cofactors; rounded values are used only
for rendering the curves. (B) D₃G_ε(Q)/D₃F(Q) and D₄G_ε(Q)/D₄F(Q).
The lines nearly coincide but their certified coefficients are distinct.
Both ratios exceed the common bound 0.7995. Exact moment annihilation
keeps Q a triple zero, and the derivative bounds retain cusp
nondegeneracy. This figure concerns one selected direction and one
fixed cusp; the large amplitude result is not a whole-arc theorem.
[PNG](figures/pinned_direction.png), [SVG](figures/pinned_direction.svg).

The figures are explanatory artifacts and are not proof inputs. The
SVG versions provide vector graphics for a prospective manuscript.
No new PDF or fully compiled manuscript is part of this package.
