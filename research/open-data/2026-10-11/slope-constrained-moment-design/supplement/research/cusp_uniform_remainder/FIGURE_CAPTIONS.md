# R27 figure caption

**Uniform positivity and numerical variation along a short theta cusp
arc.** The horizontal axis uses 10⁶ν on the exact interval [−2⁻¹⁶,0].
Left: the band 2.47×10⁻³⁹<C₄(ν)<2.52×10⁻³⁹ is proved for every
point of the interval. Its flat boundaries are common enclosing constants,
not a claim that C₄ is constant or that the band is its exact range.
The dots are separate numerical values. Right: the same five numerical
values are magnified relative to C₄(0), in parts per million. They are
obtained by direct original-integral cusp and dual solves, not by evaluating
the uniform bound as if it were an optimum. Their visible increase is
diagnostic; monotonicity on the continuum is not proved. The accompanying
uniform remainder theorem applies for all M≥2×10⁻⁵ with coefficients
depending on ν and a separate design at each cusp. The figure does not
represent the whole [−29,0] cusp curve.
