# Dependencies and literature scope

This is a numerical continuation of the existing research question, not
a fresh literature-priority investigation. It adds no physical application
claim. The preceding discussion of sectors did not change the problem.

Primary internal mathematical dependencies:

1. [R11 proof](../kernel_design_principle/PROOF.md): fixed-Q moment target,
   right inverses and minimum relative amplitude formulation.
2. [R12 proof](../kernel_norm_threshold/PROOF.md): exact fixed residual,
   sign/root cover, signed moments, global q_n Lipschitz constants,
   logistic smoothing and nonsingular correction matrix.
3. [R13 proof](../kernel_slope_budget/PROOF.md): finite slope problem,
   separated-transition derivative bound and paired local loss inequality.
4. [R13 literature scope](../kernel_slope_budget/REFERENCES.md): sources
   already read for the earlier general compactness/convexity arguments.

R14 derives its truncated smoothing identity and its summed-loss bound
explicitly in [PROOF.md](PROOF.md). No claim is made that convolution,
linear moment correction, Cramer's rule or summing losses over disjoint
sets is a new general mathematical method. The possible contribution is
the quantitative certified result for this positive integral problem.
Independent mathematical review and a focused priority review remain open.
