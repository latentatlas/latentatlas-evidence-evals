# Figure — Quantified increase of the fourth coefficient

Left: bounds for 10⁶[C₄(ν)/C₄(0)−1] on −2⁻¹⁶≤ν≤0, obtained
by integrating the uniform derivative enclosure and using R24's exact
endpoint enclosure. For ν≤0 the lower and upper bounds are respectively
10⁶ν(4.35×10⁻⁴⁰)/(2.49203004×10⁻³⁹) and
10⁶ν(2.82×10⁻⁴⁰)/(2.49203006×10⁻³⁹). Both vanish at the anchor.
The orange points are 13 separately solved numerical coefficient values,
normalized by their own numerical endpoint value. They are diagnostic.

Right: the proved common interval for 10⁴⁰C₄'(ν), which is strictly
above zero on the whole arc. Three orange points are finite differences
of separately solved original-integral problems with step 2⁻²¹; they
are not certified pointwise derivative enclosures. The constant-width
green band does not say that the derivative is constant.

The graph concerns the original ν coordinate and this short arc only.
It does not show the true finite-M optimal value or prove its monotonicity.
PNG and SVG are standalone publication-preparation artifacts. Their data
and hashes are in [plot_data.json](results/plot_data.json); their visual
inspection is recorded in [figure_qa.json](results/figure_qa.json).
