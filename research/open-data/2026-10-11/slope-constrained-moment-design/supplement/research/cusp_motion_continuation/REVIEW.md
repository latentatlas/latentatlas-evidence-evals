# R29 — Review record and trust boundary

The producer constructs new cusp anchors, identifies them inside an existing
uniqueness tube, transports preconditioned sign residuals and proves a unique
dual for every driver in each cell. Neighboring solutions are identified
mathematically by uniqueness, rather than by a plot or overlapping boxes.
Infinite theta and root tails are retained in the stated derivative theorem.

The new analytic issues are the one-sided domination of positive moments,
the integral preconditioned residual bound, the objective's quadratic anchor
error, and the sextic extension of the R23 weighted summability condition.
They are derived in [PROOF.md](PROOF.md). Finite-M error constants from R27
are deliberately not a consequence of this calculation.

An independent outward-rational pass reconstructs the local Newton
contractions, dual contraction and tightening inequalities, rank decisions,
root containment, sign inequalities and matrix solves. It also independently
differentiates the coefficient formula using forward automatic differentiation.
It accepts the enumerated transcendental interval inputs, fixed-b derivative
rhs and certain interval sums; those are regenerated by the Arb producer.
The 17.088 checks are not 17.088 independent mathematical theorems or a
measure of publication quality. They are traceable numerical decisions.

The mpmath implementation re-solves the original cusp and dual moment
equations at 23 drivers, with two precisions and two finite-difference
steps. These calculations are diagnostic because their omitted domain,
series and finite-difference errors are not enclosed there. Their agreement
corroborates the separate analytic/interval proof; it does not replace it.

Fresh `--with-arb` reproduction reconstructs all 32 anchors and cells in an
unrelated temporary directory and compares exact output bytes. All 29 earlier
manifests and 1162 file entries are checked and the R28 audit is rerun.
The archive's portable verifier is additionally run after export.

The two substantive comparisons are: (a) 1024-fold increase in the proved
driver length, and (b) a failed wide enclosure resolved on exactly the same
driver interval by subdivision. The latter is evidence about numerical
overestimation, not evidence that the continuation reaches its maximum extent.

This package has no external referee review, formal proof, established
literature priority, new physical interpretation, degree equivalence verdict
or submission. Its result is a bounded coefficient-sensitivity theorem
conditional on the explicit analytic proof and the inherited theorem chain.
