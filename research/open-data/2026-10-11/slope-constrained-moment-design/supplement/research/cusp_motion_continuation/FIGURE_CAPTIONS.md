# R29 — Figure caption

**Continuation of the fourth asymptotic coefficient on the exact cusp.**
(A) Relative coefficient change from ν=0. The green region follows from
the uniform derivative bounds and the rigorous endpoint coefficient
enclosure. Orange points come from independent 110-digit original-integral
solutions and are diagnostic. (B) The blue band is the union of the
individual validated derivative intervals, shown over each of the 32 closed
driver cells. It stays strictly above zero. Orange points are two-step
finite-difference diagnostics; the band is not a fit to these points.
(C) Two interval calculations over the same driver range [−1/512,0].
The single wide box cannot decide the derivative's sign; four recentered
small boxes prove it positive everywhere there. Bars are deterministic
enclosures, not confidence intervals. The original u and ν coordinates are
used. This figure does not claim convexity of C₄, monotonicity of the true
finite-M optimum, or extension of the earlier finite-M remainder constants.
