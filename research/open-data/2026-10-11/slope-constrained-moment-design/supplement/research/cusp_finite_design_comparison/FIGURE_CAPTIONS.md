# Figure — Finite-budget design comparisons

Panels A and B show rigorous enclosures for differences from the driver
ν=−1/64 at M₀=2×10⁻⁵, derived from Theorem 2. A bounds the actual
optimal perturbation amplitude. B removes the first two exact terms and
rescales by M₀⁴, thereby isolating the fourth-order contribution within
its proved remainder. The filled regions are allowed value ranges, not
empirical confidence intervals or fitted optimum curves. At the reference
driver the true difference is exactly zero; the general two-value error
bound in B remains conservative there.

Panel C shows sufficient driver separation for strict ordering, versus
M/M₀. The green bound scales as M⁻⁶ and the blue as M⁻². The orange
line is the fixed spacing 1/2048 of 33 ordered drivers. Both criteria
hold for this spacing at all M≥M₀, including budgets beyond the plotted
range. No monotonicity at arbitrarily close drivers follows from these
value bounds. All axes use the original parameter normalization.

The mathematical data in `results/plot_data.json` are exact rational
values. Floating-point conversion is used only to render the figure.
PNG and SVG are provided for a future manuscript, subject to independent
review of the inherited analytic chain.
