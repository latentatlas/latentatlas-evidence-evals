# Figure caption

**Positive fourth-order coefficient at the fixed theta cusp.** Left:
the three terms δ₀⁵Γ²/(3D²), −δ₀⁵R/D and δ₀⁵P/D, and their total
C₄, in units of 10^{-39}. All use the same exact base problem; the
moment term is not a comparison obtained by removing moment constraints.
Bar values show interval midpoints; certified widths are below visual
resolution. Right: the ratio (C₄/M⁴)/(C₂/M²) over the displayed budgets.
The red point is its arithmetic evaluation at M=2×10^{-5}. This panel
plots the relative magnitude of known asymptotic terms, not an enclosed
finite-budget optimum or an error bound. The existence of a positive C₄
does imply eventual underestimation by the leading correction, but a
numerical onset is not provided by this package.
