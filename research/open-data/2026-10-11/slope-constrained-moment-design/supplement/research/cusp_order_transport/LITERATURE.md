# Limited background check, 23 September 2026

The proof is self-contained relative to the frozen cusp and feasibility
packages. Two primary sources were consulted to place its tools:

- Arafat Abbar, Clément Coine and Colin Petitjean,
  [A pre-adjoint approach on weighted composition operators between spaces of Lipschitz functions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.12173),
  2023. Its abstract explicitly studies operators g↦w·g∘f on Lipschitz
  spaces and norm bounds. We consulted the abstract; no theorem from
  its full text is being invoked to certify the present noncompact-domain
  operator or exact moment constraints.
- Stanford Math 220A,
  [First-Order Equations: Method of Characteristics](https://web.stanford.edu/class/math220a/handouts/firstorder.pdf),
  pp. 1–3 of the handout. This explains the standard reduction of a
  first-order PDE to ordinary differential equations along characteristics.
  R31 supplies its own characteristic and absolute-value comparison.

Inference: weighted composition and characteristics are established
mathematical tools; claiming either as a new general method would be
unjustified. R31's specific content is the explicit moment-preserving
weight, a sufficient contraction inequality, and its whole-domain
validation for the exact theta cusp family. This limited check does not
establish that this particular result is absent from the literature.

Before a journal submission, compare the complete proposed theorem
with work on parameter comparison of moment optimization under bounded
Lipschitz constraints, rather than rely on keyword search or unfamiliarity.
No priority or publication verdict is made here.
