# Figure R31

**Strict ordering without a minimum parameter separation.** Panel A shows
outward rational upper bounds for r+κ+a₀|rᵤ| on 1,024 complete u cells,
with all cusp parameters simultaneously enclosed. They lie below −0.02;
the entire u≥1 tail is covered analytically, with upper bound <−0.0511.
The curve joins cell upper bounds for display; the underlying proof
uses each complete interval. Panel B compares the old remainder-based
lower bound with the transport lower bound for
[δ_{ν₂}(M₀)−δ_{ν₁}(M₀)]/(ν₂−ν₁), scaled by 10⁻¹¹. The new lower bound
is 2.07 for every positive separation; the displayed axis is finite,
while the proof covers all d>0 with ν₁,ν₂∈[−1/64,0]. The old bound
remains sharper at sufficiently large separations. Neither curve is a
computed exact optimizer. The normalized fourth-order residual remains
a distinct question. PNG and vector SVG are supplied.
