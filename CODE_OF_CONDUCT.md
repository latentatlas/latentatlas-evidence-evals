# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a professional, respectful, and technically rigorous
environment for everyone who participates in this project.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss evidence, implementation, and tradeoffs directly and respectfully.
- Give feedback that is specific, reproducible, and actionable.
- Credit the work and ideas of others.
- Protect private, sensitive, and security-relevant information.
- Make space for questions and different levels of experience.

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, deliberate disruption, and the
publication of another person's private information are unacceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Concerns may be reported privately to `huseyin@latentatlas.ai`. Reports will be
reviewed promptly and handled with appropriate confidentiality. Maintainers may
remove content or restrict participation when behavior undermines the safety or
integrity of the project.
